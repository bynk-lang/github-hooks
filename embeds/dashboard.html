Pass rate is passed ÷ (passed + failed + timed out). Wall-clock time runs from when CI was triggered to when it finished, including waiting for runners, over passed runs. A run is flaky when its first attempt failed and a re-run passed. Recent runs are each workflow's latest 20 completed attempts in the window, oldest on the left; runs not counted in the pass rate are drawn half height. Trends are by UTC day. A job's wait for a runner runs from when it was queued to when a runner took it, over jobs that started in the window; jobs held for an environment's approval, and skipped jobs, aren't counted. Runner waits and slowest jobs are computed hourly; a job is among the slowest by its median time over successful runs, with at least 3 in the window. What's waiting now counts jobs queued in the last day. Data: /metrics/ci, /metrics/ci/now, /metrics/ci/history, /metrics/runners, /metrics/runners/now. Source: bynk-lang/github-hooks, written in Bynk.
