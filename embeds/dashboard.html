Pass rate is passed ÷ (passed + failed + timed out). Wall-clock time runs from when CI was triggered to when it finished, including waiting for runners, over passed runs. A run is flaky when its first attempt failed and a re-run passed. Recent runs are each workflow's latest 20 completed attempts in the window, oldest on the left; runs not counted in the pass rate are drawn half height. Trends are by UTC day. Data: /metrics/ci, /metrics/ci/now, /metrics/ci/history. Source: bynk-lang/github-hooks, written in Bynk.
